# Impact

This is a substantive purpose, scope, data-role, route, stage, gate, outcome, and terminal-authority
change for the existing successor draft. It deliberately turns v009 into a fixed historical
research and execution-rehearsal protocol rather than a reusable path to prospective Shadow or
controlled live activation.

The active v008 release remains unchanged and authoritative. Its existing studies are not moved,
executed, repaired, or reinterpreted: v008/S001 and v008/S002 remain completed; paused v008/S003
keeps the accepted `continue-on-v008` decision and is not resumed or copied into v009. Their exact
frozen calendars and outcomes remain governed by v008.

The existing v009 draft must be regenerated only after this change receives a stable human
decision. Its combined impact review must continue to cover accepted v008/C001-C004 while adding
this change as a direct source. Challenge-only execution, categorized result storage, workflow
release activation, and release-safety persistence remain applicable governance and evidence
infrastructure, but v009 study outcomes no longer feed Shadow, Controlled Activation, Active
monitoring, broker, order, or live authority.

Implementation must add a versioned fixed-calendar route/schema or equivalent exact-version
compiler contract, reject all caller calendar overrides, bind the complete session inventories,
and provide a guarded provider-free historical execution-replay operation. Existing v001-v008
workflow and study replay must remain byte-identical and retain their original route and calendar
semantics.

The fixed dates exclude assets without complete required history. Such assets cannot shorten,
shift, or partially populate the calendar and must be rejected before outcome-relevant execution.
This trades broad asset eligibility and freshness for exact cross-study comparability and immutable
version-level time boundaries.

The current prospective qualification, cutover, ledger, and drift documents may remain referenced
for reusable execution and evidence mechanics, but v009 must explicitly narrow their authority to
the historical replay. Any normative wording that requires prospective registration or enables
promotion must not be imported as v009 study authority.

No release, activation, study transition, study execution, broker access, order action, commit,
push, or merge is authorized by this proposal.
