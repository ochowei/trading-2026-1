## Rationale

clean. Asset-specific clean-evidence classification preserves the existing Historical boundary,
while the bounded `retrospectively-supported` status makes useful negative or supporting evidence
recordable without granting qualification. The replacement must make retrospective evidence
structurally unable to grant Historical, Shadow, activation, or live authority and must fully
support workflow-native qualification identities.
