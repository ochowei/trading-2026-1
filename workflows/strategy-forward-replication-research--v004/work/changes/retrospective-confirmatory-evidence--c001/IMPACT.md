# Impact

## Rules and artifacts affected

The replacement workflow must update purpose and decision language, entry conditions, data-role
classification, stage ordering, invariants, required evidence, outcomes, stopping behavior, version
boundaries, and shared implementation links. It must distinguish the four concepts
`Development`, `retrospective-confirmatory`, clean `Historical Evaluation`, and prospective
`Shadow` without allowing session-role overlap or retrospective relabeling.

The normative Historical/Shadow contract and result-validity documentation require a compatible
retrospective plan, screen, evidence-status, and replay contract. The qualification registry and CLI
require workflow-native plan/screen support based on exact research-definition identities rather
than `get_experiment()` from the closed legacy inventory. Result schemas, trial-family verification,
definition capture, immutable manifest validation, status projection, tests, and architecture
documentation may be affected. Released v004 files, policies, studies, results, and registries
remain untouched.

The next-version contract must retain v004's exact policy selections unless a separately accepted
change justifies a policy replacement. It must preserve all current Historical, Shadow,
activation, monitoring, auxiliary-lag, observation-provenance, broker, and no-live-authorization
guards.

## Existing studies and hypotheses

- v004 S001, S002, and S003 are terminal and remain immutable; no disposition is required.
- v004 S004 is paused after an approved candidate freeze. Its disposition is
  `continue-on-v004`: its preregistered 2027-2031 future-only Historical boundary, candidate,
  baseline, gates, and evidence remain unchanged. This change does not authorize S004 Historical
  access, robustness execution, plan registration, outcome inspection, or migration.

After v005 becomes effective, a retrospective FXI study would be a new CLI-allocated local study
under v005 and may identify the exact v004 S004 path through `revisits`. It must have its own
hypothesis, plan, human preregistration approval, candidate selection/freeze evidence, and stage
authorizations. It cannot import S004's candidate-freeze approval as approval of a new outcome
inspection, and it cannot overwrite or revive S003 or S004.

## Compatibility and migration risk

The principal scientific risk is label leakage: users or downstream systems may treat
`retrospectively-supported` as Historical validation or qualification. The replacement must make
the status non-promotional by construction and reject Shadow registration or activation evidence
that cites only a retrospective screen.

The principal selection risk is using a retrospective result to repair the candidate and then
reporting the same periods as confirmation. Immutable plans, append-only trial history, exact
session-role inventories, and new-study requirements after outcome-informed changes must fail
closed against this behavior.

The principal compatibility risk is extending qualification persistence and result schemas while
preserving old registry verification. Readers must continue to validate existing events exactly;
new event types or schema versions require explicit compatibility tests and cannot synthesize
retrospective status for legacy results.

The principal operational risk is the current legacy-only qualification registration path. v005
must not become effective unless workflow-native research definitions can register and replay both
the new checkpoint and clean Historical screens with exact policy, source, family, and manifest
identities.
