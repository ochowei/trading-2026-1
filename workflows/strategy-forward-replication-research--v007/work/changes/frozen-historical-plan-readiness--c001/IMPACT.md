# Impact

## Rules and artifacts affected

This change affects planning/readiness stages, clean-Historical data-role representation,
candidate-freeze evidence retention, complete-family selection-boundary registration, and
release-readiness validation. Expected authoritative implementation work includes the qualification
domain model, workflow coordinator, registry/CLI surfaces, provider-free tests, the versioned Phase
6 normative contract, reproducibility documentation where necessary, and architecture documentation
for any new tracked artifact pattern.

It does not change strategy definitions, parameters, trial budget, signal or execution semantics,
costs, thresholds, folds, outcome rules, retrospective labels, promotion authority, or S004's
frozen study artifacts. Because it changes outcome-relevant qualification planning and evidence
requirements, it requires an accepted change and replacement workflow version rather than a v007
README erratum.

## Existing studies and hypotheses

- There are no unfinished studies under v007.
- The only unfinished study in the workflow family is
  `strategy-forward-replication-research@v004/S004`. Its existing disposition remains
  `continue-on-v004`: it stays paused, pinned to v004, and retains its exact 2015-2025 Development,
  2026 quarantine, and 2027-2031 Historical boundaries. This change does not migrate, copy,
  preregister, resume, or authorize it.
- All completed and cancelled studies remain immutable. No prior Historical or retrospective
  evidence may be rerun, relabeled, or repaired through a new family identity.

Before the first 2027 Evaluation outcome begins, S004 requires a separate study-operation approval
to register its exact Historical plan after the repaired provider-free path has passed independent
review. The operation remains governed by v004 and S004's frozen artifacts, not by replacement
workflow semantics. If the compatibility tooling cannot express those exact existing rules without
adding authority, S004 remains paused. Change acceptance or release alone grants no authority to
register, resume, execute, inspect outcomes, or reinterpret the study.

## Compatibility and migration risk

Legacy plans using the standard preceding-three-year chronology must retain their current wire
identity and behavior. Explicit clean calendars require a distinct, deterministic representation;
old payloads must never acquire synthetic role inventories during reload.

The primary scientific risks are accidental use of quarantined sessions, incomplete family freeze,
or post-boundary trial registration. The preservation risk is treating locally reachable but
untracked Git objects as durable evidence. The operational risks are allowing dry-run compilation
to mutate registries, allowing register-only preparation to execute definitions, or making a
compatibility repair appear to authorize Historical access. Public-path tests and independent
review must challenge each boundary. The content-addressed evidence store must also reject digest
mismatch, overwrite, mutable aliases, missing fresh-clone artifacts, and retention that depends on
unreachable Git objects.
