# Qualify strategies under base and stress execution costs

Each research definition preregisters a base execution cost policy and an adverse stress-cost scenario, and historical, shadow, and portfolio simulations report gross, base-net, and stress-net outcomes. Qualification requires all normal thresholds under base costs and positive cumulative return with profit factor above one under stress; confirmed fills feed execution drift and may inform a later trial but cannot retune the frozen definition. This rejects advantages that disappear under modestly worse real-world execution.
